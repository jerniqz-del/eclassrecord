# Facebook Post - E-Class Record v1.4.0

E-Class Record App v1.4.0 is now ready with a new Attendance Tracker.

This update adds a dedicated Attendance Tracker designed to help teachers document absences and tardiness directly inside the app without changing grades or class record computations.

What's New:

- Record absences and tardiness in the Attendance Tracker
- View a monthly attendance table with weekends excluded
- Take attendance through a Roll Call modal where everyone starts as Present
- Mark Tardy or Absent with clear color-coded buttons
- Review attendance analytics by learner and date range
- Spot learners with repeated monthly absences through row highlights
- Mark attendance records as Open, Monitoring, or Resolved
- Filter attendance by learner, term, type, status, and follow-ups due
- Print or download Attendance Logs as PDF
- Open attendance logs directly from the class roster or learner grade report
- Download parent-ready Term Completion Reports for individual learners with pending outputs/tests
- Works offline and stays teacher-owned

Important note: grades, formulas, scores, summaries, and assessment calculations are unchanged.
