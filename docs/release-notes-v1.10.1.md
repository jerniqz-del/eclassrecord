# E-Class Record v1.10.1

Release date: September 10, 2026

## Attendance Tracker

- Take Roll Call opens again from the Attendance Tracker.
- Clicking a date on the monthly grid opens roll call for that day.
- The week date strip in the roll-call window updates when you choose another date.

## Compatibility

- Existing profiles, grades, attendance marks, no-class days, backups, Advisory records, and formulas remain compatible.
- This update does not rewrite stored attendance or scores.
