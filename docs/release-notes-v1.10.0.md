# E-Class Record v1.10.0

Release date: September 9, 2026

## K to 10 Updated ECR

- The app now follows the 2026-09-08 K to 10 Updated ECR, Grade 1 PACE, and SF9 official packs for daily class-record work.
- Print and sheet headers use Written/Oral Works, Product/Performance Tasks, and Examinations.
- Filipino SF9 descriptors use the official wording: *Naipamamalas* and *Nagpapaunlad*.
- New Grades 2–3 numeric classes start with 5 Written/Oral Works and 3 Product/Performance Tasks. Existing 4+4 classes stay 4+4.

## Grade 1 PACE and Kindergarten

- Grade 1 uses the PACE booklet for A–E rating, with Individual and Class modes, median/override letters, and Android companion rating.
- The competency list shows each item once, with nested live skills and a./b. subcompetencies.
- The Term 1 Individual PACE Form prints a labeled School field from Settings.
- Kindergarten is available as a teaching load and advisory grade with A–E developmental rating. It does not use WW/PT or transmutation.

## Official export and mid-year sheet changes

- Export Official ECR fills the official Academic, GMRC/Values, MAPEH, EPP/TLE, Grade 1, and Kinder workbooks from Settings and the roster. Generic Excel export remains.
- GMRC/Values can use official domain scoring (WW Cognitive/Affective and PT Cognitive/Affective/Behavioral). Existing GMRC stays pooled WW/PT until you opt in.
- TLE can use per-component scoring (ICT 25% plus AFA/FCS/IA 75% by term). Existing TLE stays single-component until you opt in.
- Duplicate to official sheet copies learners and Term 1 finals onto a new official-layout class for Terms 2–3. The source class is unchanged.

## Strengthened Senior High School (DepEd Order No. 017, s. 2026)

- Grade 11 classes use the Strengthened SHS subject catalog: five cores, Academic and TechPro electives, and no strands.
- Grade 12 in SY 2026-2027 uses the 2016 K to 12 SHS subjects by default, with DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015 component weights.
- Check **This Grade 12 class uses Strengthened SHS (pilot school)** only if the school started SSHS in SY 2025-2026.
- Grade 12 from SY 2027-2028 uses Strengthened SHS for all schools.

## Desktop and Android reliability

- The 6-digit profile PIN unlocks automatically after the sixth digit on desktop and Android. Unlock buttons remain as a fallback.
- Linked Android Quick Grade stays on the current learner when a live desktop snapshot arrives.

## Compatibility

- Existing profiles, grades, attendance, assessments, backups, Advisory records, and formulas remain compatible.
- This update does not rewrite stored raw scores, delete assessment columns, or switch existing GMRC or TLE classes by subject name.
- Existing Grade 1 PACE letters stay on the one-letter keys. Term grades continue to be calculated from existing scores and the official tables.
