# Facebook Post - E-Class Record v1.10.0

Copy below this line for Facebook. Covers improvements from v1.9.6 through v1.10.0.

---

📢 E-Class Record App v1.10.0 is now available!

If you are still on v1.9.6, this update includes everything added in v1.9.7, v1.9.8, and v1.10.0. Existing records, grades, backups, and formulas stay compatible. Stored scores are not rewritten.

🌟 K to 10 Updated ECR, Grade 1 PACE, and Kinder
• Matches the official K to 10 Updated ECR, Grade 1 PACE, and SF9 packs
• Grade 1 PACE booklet for A–E rating, with Individual and Class modes
• Nested Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Copying lines on the competency list
• Individual PACE Form now prints a labeled School field from Settings
• Kindergarten is available as a teaching load and advisory grade with A–E rating
• Export Official ECR fills the official DepEd workbooks from Settings and your roster
• GMRC can use official domain scoring, and TLE can use ICT + specialization components — existing classes stay on their current sheet until you choose to switch
• Duplicate to official sheet copies learners and Term 1 finals onto a new class for Terms 2–3
• New Grades 2–3 classes start with 5 Written/Oral Works and 3 Product/Performance Tasks; existing 4+4 classes stay 4+4
• Filipino SF9 wording is now Naipamamalas and Nagpapaunlad

🎓 Strengthened Senior High School (DepEd Order No. 017, s. 2026)
• Grade 11 uses the Strengthened SHS curriculum
• Grade 12 in SY 2026-2027 stays on the 2016 K to 12 subjects by default
• Pilot schools can mark a Grade 12 class as Strengthened SHS
• Grade 12 from SY 2027-2028 uses Strengthened SHS automatically
• 2016 Grade 12 classes keep DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015 weights with the DO 15 transmutation table

🔍 Transmutation table and safer desktop profiles
• Click any transmuted grade to open the official transmutation table, with the matching range highlighted
• Initial grades show two decimal places (example: 24.00); transmuted grades stay whole numbers
• Manage desktop profiles in the app, including restore from a profile backup
• Deleting a profile now requires saving a complete backup first
• If Windows locks, your E-Class Record profile stays locked until you unlock it
• If the app stops responding, you can wait, reload safely, or exit without wiping records
• The 6-digit PIN unlocks automatically after the last digit
• Linked Android Quick Grade stays on the current learner when live grades arrive from the desktop

💻 How to get the update
• New users: https://github.com/jerniqz-del/eclassrecord/releases/download/v1.10.0/E-Class-Record-Setup-1.10.0.exe
• Existing users: open the app → Settings → Check for Updates

Please share this with fellow teachers. Thank you for using E-Class Record!
