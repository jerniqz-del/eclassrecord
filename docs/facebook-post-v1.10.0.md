# Facebook Post - E-Class Record v1.10.0

E-Class Record App v1.10.0 is now available as a public update.

This update matches the K to 10 Updated ECR, Grade 1 PACE, and SF9 official packs, and includes Strengthened Senior High School subjects from DepEd Order No. 017, s. 2026.

What's new:

- Grade 1 PACE booklet for A–E rating, with a labeled School field on the Individual PACE Form
- Kindergarten A–E developmental rating
- Export Official ECR fills the official DepEd workbooks
- Optional official GMRC domain scoring and TLE component scoring; existing classes stay on their current sheet
- New Grades 2–3 classes use 5 Written/Oral Works and 3 Product/Performance Tasks
- Grade 11 uses Strengthened SHS; Grade 12 in SY 2026-2027 stays on the 2016 catalog unless it is a pilot class

Existing records, grades, backups, and formulas remain compatible.
